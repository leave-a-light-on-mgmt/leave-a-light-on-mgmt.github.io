Jeweler

Bio

On their debut LP Tiny Circles, Minneapolis-based quintet Jeweler mine the sonic palettes of lush U.K. shoegaze, melodic noise-pop, and expansive, psych-tinged art rock to build a musical vocabulary both uniquely their own and eminently engaging.

Grown out of the bedroom studio project of Michael Voller, the group has expanded to include Sylvia Jennings (vocals and keyboard), Dillon Marchus (bass), Sean Levine (lead guitar), and Lars Oslund (drums).

Voller grew up immersed in music, playing and writing from an early age. He was also drawn to the sciences, and in college he studied Aerospace Engineering. In time, he turned his scientific mind to the process of recording. He studied synthesizers and sound design, and meticulously analyzed the songwriting and production techniques of songs he loved as a fan. Together, Jeweler have crafted a record of breadth and nuance, arresting and fully wrought.
